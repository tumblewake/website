Email support

What to put in your message

The more we know, the faster we can help. If you can, include:

  • Your iPhone model, for example “iPhone 13”.
  • Your iOS version. You can find it in Settings › General › About.
  • What happened, and what you expected to happen instead.
  • What you were doing at the time: the mode, the wrestler and the arena.
  • Whether it happens every time, or only once in a while.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you have one.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sumo Arena out yet?

Not yet. It is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone as “Sumo Arena - Dohyo Showdown”. On your Home Screen it is called “Sumo Arena”.

Is it free? Are there ads or purchases?

It is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. Nothing in it costs real money: kensho, gold and diamonds are earned only by playing.

Do I need the internet or an account?

No. Sumo Arena works entirely offline, with no account and no sign-in. Open the game and play.

How do I play?

Drag the left half of the screen to move, and shove the others off the floor. Your two abilities and Boost are the buttons at the bottom right. Settings › How to Play shows it again.

Who am I playing against?

Every match is against bots. You can set how tough they are under Settings › Bot difficulty: Easy, Normal, Hard or Yokozuna.

How do I get chests?

Play matches to earn kensho. Every 1,000 kensho opens a chest, from Wood to Diamond, with gold every time and sometimes diamonds or a new look. The free Season Pass gives more as you play.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your iPhone: your kensho, gold, diamonds, chests, looks, Shop items, Season Pass tier and World Tournament. We hold no copy, so if you delete the app we cannot bring them back.

Does Sumo Arena collect my data?

No. There is no tracking and no analytics, and the game sends nothing to us. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Still stuck?

Email support@tumblewake.com and tell us what is going on.
