Support

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Send us a message and we will help.

Email us

Write to support@tumblewake.com for help with Sumo Arena or anything else about TumbleWake Games. TumbleWake is a one-person studio, so you will hear back from the person who made the game.

Email support

What to put in your message

The more we know, the faster we can help. If you can, include:

  • Your device, for example “iPhone 13” or “iPad Air”.
  • Your iOS or iPadOS version. You can find it in Settings › General › About.
  • What happened, and what you expected to happen instead.
  • What you were doing at the time: the mode, the wrestler and the arena.
  • Whether it happens every time, or only once in a while.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you have one.
Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Sumo Arena out yet?

Not yet. Sumo Arena is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone. On your Home Screen it will be called “Sumo Arena”.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Sumo Arena works entirely offline, so you can play anywhere.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-in. Open the game and play.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Your kensho, gold, diamonds, chests, shields, looks, Season Pass tier and World Tournament are kept in files on your iPhone or iPad. We do not have a copy.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting Sumo Arena deletes your settings and progress with it. Because we hold no copy, we cannot bring them back.

Who am I playing against?

Every match is against bots. You can set how tough they are under Settings › Bot difficulty: Easy, Normal, Hard or Yokozuna.

Does Sumo Arena collect my data or show ads?

No. Sumo Arena collects no data and shows no ads. There is no tracking and no analytics. Read the privacy policy for the details.

How do I get chests?

Play matches to earn kensho. Every 1,000 kensho opens a chest, from Wood to Diamond, with gold, sometimes diamonds and sometimes a new look. Spend your gold and diamonds in the Shop. The free Season Pass also hands out gold, diamonds, chests and three outfits to earn as you play.

Still stuck?

Email support@tumblewake.com and tell us what is going on.
