Sumo Arena

Shove them off the ring. A fast arcade brawler: you are a ball, they are balls, and the last one left on the floor wins.

On your Home Screen the game is simply called Sumo.

Five ways to play Support

Free for all: four players, five rounds, and a floor that keeps shrinking.

How it plays

Last one on the floor wins

Pick a wrestler, take two abilities in, and shove everyone else off a shrinking arena. Hit them in the back and they fly further. Land hits to fill your Boost, then tap it when it turns gold.

Chad

Heavy and slow

Brings a grappling Hook.

Bloke

The all-rounder

Brings the S-Gun.

Shrew

Light and quick

Brings the Dash.

Modes

Five ways to play

1v1

Duel

You against one rival, first to five rounds.

4

Free for all

Four players, five rounds, points for the last ones standing.

16

Free for all 16

Sixteen players on one huge wheel. Last one standing wins.

2v2

Teams

You and a partner against two rivals.

World Tournament

32 players in eight groups of four, then a bracket all the way to the final.

Every match is against bots on Easy, Normal, Hard or Yokozuna. They are always ready, so there is no waiting for anyone.

Progress

Level up

Play matches to earn kensho. Open chests, from Wood to Diamond, for gold and diamonds, then level each of the five abilities from 1 to 10.

  • Shield
  • Jump
  • Hook
  • S-Gun
  • Dash
