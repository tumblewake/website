Sumo Arena

Fast arcade sumo: shove your rivals off the floor. Last one standing wins.

Five ways to play Support

Free for all: 4 players, 5 rounds, one shrinking floor.

How it plays

Last one on the floor wins

Pick a wrestler and two of three abilities: their own, the Shield or the Jump. Then shove your rivals off a shrinking floor. Back hits launch further. Land hits to fill Boost, then tap it at gold: briefly heavier and faster.

Chad

Heavyweight

Hardest to shove, but easiest to hit.

Hook: Chad hooks a wrestler, crashes in, slides on.

Bloke

All-rounder

Middle weight, middle speed, shoves from range.

S-Gun: Bloke blasts a wrestler away, hardest up close.

Shrew

Speedster

The quickest, but loses head-on shoves.

Dash: Shrew dashes twice, hitting hard even head-on.

Shield: halves shoves and stops S-Gun bolts from the front. Jump: hop over charging rivals; nothing can touch you in the air.

World Tournament

32 players, 8 groups. Get through yours, then win every knockout!

Four arenas: Dohyo, Beach, Lava and Carnival. Every match is against bots: Easy, Normal, Hard or Yokozuna, sumo’s grand champion. No waiting.

Progress

Chests, looks and a free Season Pass

Finish higher, earn more kensho. Every 1,000 kensho opens a chest, Wood to Diamond: gold every time, sometimes a look or diamonds.

Spend gold and diamonds in the Shop on the Scutum shield or the day’s three looks (outfits, belts and hats), then dress up in the Locker.

The Season Pass is free: 40 tiers of gold, diamonds, chests and three outfits to earn. New season every 28 days.
