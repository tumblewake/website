Sumo Arena

Fast arcade sumo: shove your rivals off the floor. Last one standing wins.

Five ways to play Support

Free for all: 4 players, 5 rounds, one shrinking floor.

How it plays

Last one on the floor wins

Pick a wrestler and two abilities, then shove your rivals off a shrinking floor. Back hits launch further. Land hits to fill Boost, then tap it at gold: briefly heavier and faster.

Chad

Heavyweight

Hardest to shove, but easiest to hit.

Hook: Chad hooks a wrestler, crashes in, slides on.

Bloke

All-rounder

Middle weight, middle speed, shoves from range.

S-Gun: Bloke blasts a wrestler away, hardest up close.

Shrew

Speedster

The quickest, but loses head-on shoves.

Dash: Shrew dashes twice, hitting hard even head-on.

World Tournament

32 players, 8 groups. Get through yours, then win every knockout!

Every match is against bots: Easy, Normal, Hard or Yokozuna, sumo’s grand champion. No waiting.

Progress

Chests and looks

Finish higher, earn more kensho. Every 1,000 kensho opens a chest, Wood to Diamond: gold every time, sometimes a look or diamonds. Spend gold and diamonds in the Shop on the Scutum shield or the day’s three looks.
