How we make games

A small studio with simple promises

We would rather make one game you enjoy than ten that nag you. Here is what that means for Sumo Arena.

Play anywhere

Sumo Arena works fully offline, with no account and nothing to sign in to. Play it on a plane or on the bus.

No ads, no data

Sumo Arena has no ads and no in-app purchases, and it collects no data. Your settings and progress are saved on your iPhone and are never sent to us. Read the privacy policy.

Made by a real person

TumbleWake is a one-person studio making games in Ontario, Canada. When you write to support, you are writing to the person who made the game.
