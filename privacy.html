Sumo Arena

Privacy Policy

Short version: Sumo Arena collects nothing. Your settings and progress stay on your own device and are never sent to us.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Sumo Arena is published by Tumblewake Games Inc.

What Sumo Arena stores, and where

Sumo Arena keeps your game settings (vibration, sound, music, bot difficulty, and the abilities and arena you last chose) and your game progress (kensho, gold, diamonds, ability levels, the items and looks you have unlocked, your Season Pass tier and your World Tournament) in files on your own device. That is the only place they exist, apart from any iCloud or computer backup of your device that you make yourself (we never see those backups).

What Sumo Arena collects

Nothing. Sumo Arena does not collect personal data. It sends nothing off your device to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party software development kits in the app. Sumo Arena does not use the microphone, the camera, your photos, your location or your contacts.

Purchases

If Sumo Arena offers purchases, they are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. What you bought is recorded on your device.

Children

Sumo Arena collects no data from anyone, including children.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your settings and progress stay on your device for as long as the app is installed. Deleting the app deletes all of it. We hold no copy of your game data, so there is none for us to delete or hand over.

If you email us

If you write to support@tumblewake.com, we receive your email address and whatever you put in your message, such as your device model, iOS version or a screenshot. We use it only to answer you and to fix problems in the game. We do not sell or share it, and we never add you to a mailing list. We keep it only as long as we need it to help you, and we will delete it if you ask at the same address. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian to write to us for you.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, records visitors’ IP addresses in its own logs for security. We do not receive or use those logs.

Changes to this policy

If Sumo Arena ever changes what it stores or sends, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

Support and contact

Questions or problems with Sumo Arena? Email support@tumblewake.com, or visit the support page.
